Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a grievance?

Use Submit a Grievance to provide the category, district, description and broad location when online submission opens. Online submission is currently unavailable.

How can I track my submission?

Tracking uses your ticket number and a one-time code sent to your mobile. Online tracking is currently unavailable.

Can I attach supporting documents?

You can include an optional photo or voice note with your grievance.

How will I receive updates?

Acknowledgements and status updates are sent by SMS or WhatsApp.
