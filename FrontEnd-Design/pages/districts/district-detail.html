About the District

Mysuru is a historic district in southern Karnataka, celebrated for its royal heritage, architecture and cultural traditions.

Mysuru’s palaces, historic buildings and diverse cultural landmarks reflect the region’s rich history. Administrative statistics shown above are from the district government portal; population is based on the 2011 Census.
